About Green House FreshMart

We deliver farm-fresh fruits and vegetables straight to your doorstep daily with guaranteed quality and safety.

🌱 How We Started

Green House started with a simple idea — to make fresh vegetables and fruits easily available at your doorstep. We started locally with direct customer orders through WhatsApp, focusing on freshness, quality and reliable service.

🛒 How We Purchase

We carefully select vegetables and fruits based on freshness and quality. We purchase products from trusted local farmers, growers and wholesale markets, choosing fresh produce according to customer demand.

🚚 How We Deliver

Once you place your order through our website or WhatsApp, we confirm the order, carefully pack the products and arrange doorstep delivery within our service area.
