Our Story

🌱 How We Started

Green House started with a simple idea — to make fresh vegetables and fruits easily available at your doorstep. We started locally with direct customer orders through WhatsApp, focusing on freshness, quality and reliable service.

🛒 How We Purchase

We carefully select vegetables and fruits based on freshness and quality. We purchase products from trusted local farmers, growers and wholesale markets, choosing fresh produce according to customer demand. This helps us reduce unnecessary storage and provide fresher products.

🚚 How We Deliver

Once you place your order through our website or WhatsApp, we confirm the order, carefully pack the products and arrange doorstep delivery within our service area. Our goal is to deliver your order fresh, safely and on time.

💚 Our Promise

Fresh Products • Fair Pricing • Easy Ordering • Doorstep Delivery
